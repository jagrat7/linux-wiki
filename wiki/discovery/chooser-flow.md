# Chooser Flow

Use this flow when a user has not picked an OS, desktop, or workflow yet.

## First Questions

- What are you trying to do with Linux?
- What hardware will run it?
- Do you need maximum stability, newer software, easier defaults, deep customization, or a learning-focused system?
- How comfortable are you with terminal troubleshooting?
- What operating systems or desktops have you liked before?

## Recommendation Shape

Give a short list of fits:

- Best fit
- Safer alternative
- More advanced alternative
- Option to avoid and why

## Required Tradeoffs

Always name tradeoffs around:

- Release model
- Package freshness
- Maintenance burden
- Hardware support
- Community and documentation
- Customization depth
